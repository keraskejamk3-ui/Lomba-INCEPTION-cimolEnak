SMK Telkom Malang memiliki Program Kelas Internasional (International Class Program) dengan tema "Get Ready to Become A Global Digitalent". Jumlah kursinya terbatas (limited seat).
Program Kelas Internasional Kelas 10: Building the Foundation for Global Digital Leaders. Isinya: sistem pembelajaran bilingual, pengembangan listening dan speaking intensif, produksi konten berbahasa Inggris, English Immersion Camp, serta program akademik global dan nasional terintegrasi.
Program Kelas Internasional Kelas 11: Expertise Exploration & Competency Strengthening. Isinya: sistem bilingual lanjutan, Global Communication and English Mastery Program, Overseas Academic Study Program, Global Student Exchange Program, International Certification Program, guru native English-speaking, dan program akademik global-nasional terintegrasi.
Program Kelas Internasional Kelas 12: Multiple Pathways to Success. Isinya: persiapan studi ke luar negeri, persiapan karier global, program magang internasional, persiapan masuk instansi pemerintah, dan program akademik global-nasional terintegrasi.
Keunggulan Kelas Internasional (What's in International Class): International Exposure & Global Perspective, persiapan sertifikasi internasional, Leadership & Global Mindset Coaching, Global Project & Portfolio Development, Global Future Pathway Program, Personal Career Advisor, Immersive English Learning Environment, Small Class System (kelas kecil), dan Global Strategic Communication Program.
Informasi Kelas Internasional lengkap ada di menu International Class pada website.
